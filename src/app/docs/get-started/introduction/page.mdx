# Introduction

Humanlog is the observability platform that puts developers first. Install it on your laptop and instantly get a full suite for exploring logs and distributed traces—no cloud, no extra services, no waiting for production.

## What Is Humanlog?

Humanlog brings production-grade observability right into your dev loop.
Whether you're chasing down a tricky bug in local Docker containers or validating your OpenTelemetry spans before you ship, Humanlog gives you:

- **Full insight** (logs, traces)
- **Ultra-fast iteration** on your own machine
- **Privacy** your data never leaves your machine

## Why Local-first Matters

Iteration speed is everything. Waiting 30 minutes to deploy, reproduce, and inspect is a blocker; Humanlog's localhost engine lets you:

> See, tweak, and re-run your queries in seconds—so you can catch logging anomalies, span-naming inconsistencies, or missing trace coverage before you ever merge.

## Key Benefits

1. **Speed**
   Leverage your laptop's CPU, disk, and memory for sub-second querying.

2. **Privacy**
   No third-party services, no data exfiltration. Your logs and traces stay local.

3. **Developer-first Workflow**
   A platform that works in your developer environment.
