# Concepts

> **Localhost development as a first-class citizen**;

Humanlog starts from a simple conviction: your laptop is the best place to _see_ what your code is doing. By putting observability tooling **on your machine first**, you get a zero-latency feedback loop, total privacy, and the freedom to experiment before you pollute production or pay per-byte bills.

This section gathers the core ideas that shape that experience. Think of it as the map you consult before diving into the individual deep-dives that follow.

---

## Localhost-first philosophy

Running Humanlog locally is not an after-thought or a “trial mode.”
It is the primary workflow:

- **Speed** - SSD-powered queries finish while your test suite is still compiling.
- **Waste-free** - iterate on span names and log shapes without shipping throw-away data to the cloud.
- **Privacy by default** - nothing leaves your disk unless you ask for it.

Learn why this matters, how we avoid the usual Docker-compose yak-shave, and what (little) data ever touches our servers in [**Localhost**](/docs/concepts/localhost).

## The three pillars of telemetry

| Pillar      | Mental model                           | Typical use-case                                   |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Logging** | Ordered lists of events                | Long-term forensics, security audits, BI queries   |
| **Tracing** | Directed graphs of spans (per request) | Live debugging, latency breakdown, causal analysis |
| **Metrics** | Aggregated numbers over windows        | Dashboards, alerts, SLO burn-rates                 |

### Logging

Flat lists, human-readable _and_ machine-parsable (JSON, **logfmt**). Structured logs unlock powerful search while staying grep-able, and Humanlog prettifies them so you still enjoy reading them in a terminal. Dive in → [Logging](/docs/concepts/logging).

### Tracing

Every request becomes a tree of spans with `trace_id` and `parent_span_id` glue. High volume, shorter retention, but an unbeatable microscope when you need to follow a flow end-to-end. Dive in → [Tracing](/docs/concepts/tracing).

### Metrics (coming soon)

We treat metrics as a _projection_ of logs and traces: once you have rich raw data, roll-ups are a query away. Native metrics collection and down-sampling controls will land after v1.

## Query language, storage, and scale

All pillars share the same embedded column-store beneath Humanlog. A unified pipeline-inspired language lets you slice by any field, aggregate and visualize your data.

Because it runs locally first, you can stress-test gigabyte datasets before committing to a cluster plan. When you outgrow the laptop, the same engine powers the upcoming hosted tier—no “rewrite in cloud” tax.
